Hey sparkle scientist! ✨

Imagine a tiny world made of lots of square tiles (we call them cells). Each cell can be alive (a cute pastel dot) or dead (empty). The whole world changes by a simple set of rules — no boss, just the rules!

The rules (super quick)

  • Alive + 2 or 3 friends → stays alive 💕
  • Alive + too few → lonely, it dies 😢
  • Alive + too many → crowded, it dies 😵
  • Dead + exactly 3 neighbors → new life is born ✨

Why it’s cool

  • Like pixel ecosystems that grow and dance 🌱
  • You can draw stamps and see them wiggle 🎨
  • It’s art + science at the same time 💡
